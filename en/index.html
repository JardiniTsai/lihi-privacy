This Privacy Policy explains how TS Media Group (also referred to as “lihi”, “we”, or “us”) collects, uses, retains, protects, and processes your data when you use lihi.io, app.lihi.io, knowledge.lihi.io, the lihi short-URL service, QR Codes, branded domains, click analytics, marketing link management, and other related services.

By browsing or using lihi websites and services, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please stop using the services we provide.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the websites, applications, back-end systems, knowledge base, customer-service channels, and related services operated by us, including without limitation:

  • lihi.io
  • app.lihi.io
  • knowledge.lihi.io
  • the lihi short-URL service
  • lihi QR Code and short-link management features
  • custom domains, branded domains, and related settings
  • click analytics, parameter tracking, A/B traffic splitting, and marketing analytics features
  • customer service, forms, notices, and other support services
  • the internal brand-monitoring dashboard (accessible only to authorized internal staff)

Third-party websites, third-party services, or content provided by third parties are subject to their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for how those third parties process data.

2. Data we collect

Depending on how you use our services, we may collect or process the following types of data.

2.1 Account and membership data

When you register for, sign in to, or use a lihi account, we may collect:

  • name or display name
  • email address
  • sign-in method
  • account settings
  • membership plan and subscription status
  • other information necessary for account administration

2.2 Service usage data

When you create, manage, or use lihi services, we may process:

  • short URLs you create
  • original destination URLs
  • custom domain or branded domain settings
  • QR Code-related data
  • UTM, parameters, tags, or campaign settings
  • A/B splitting or traffic-routing settings
  • link status, redirect settings, and service operation records

2.3 Click analytics and technical data

When a user clicks a short URL, scans a QR Code, or interacts with a link managed by lihi, we may process technical and analytics data, such as:

  • time of the click
  • source URL or referrer information
  • browser type
  • device type
  • operating system
  • IP address
  • approximate geographic location
  • User Agent
  • link redirect status and error records

This data is used primarily to provide click analytics, traffic analysis, security protection, abuse prevention, service maintenance, and feature improvement.

2.4 Payment and subscription data

If you use a paid plan, purchase add-on services, or buy related products, we may process data related to payments, subscriptions, invoices, or transaction records.

Actual payment information may be processed by a third-party payment service provider. We will not arbitrarily disclose your payment data, and we will retain necessary transaction records in accordance with operational and legal requirements.

2.5 Customer-service and contact data

When you contact us through a support page, form, email, or other channel, we may collect:

  • your name or how you prefer to be addressed
  • email or other contact information
  • the content of your inquiry
  • attachments, screenshots, or information you voluntarily provide
  • customer-service handling records

3. How we use data

We may use data for the following purposes:

  • providing, maintaining, and improving lihi services
  • creating, managing, and protecting user accounts
  • creating and managing short URLs, QR Codes, custom domains, and related features
  • providing click analytics, traffic analysis, A/B splitting, and marketing tracking features
  • handling subscriptions, payments, invoices, plan changes, and customer-service requests
  • sending service notices, important announcements, or necessary billing information
  • detecting, preventing, and handling fraud, abuse, phishing, malware, spam, or other unlawful activity
  • maintaining the security, stability, and performance of our services
  • complying with laws, regulators, courts, or other lawful requirements
  • enforcing our terms of service and related policies

4. Cookies and similar technologies

We may use cookies or similar technologies to keep our websites and services working properly, remember user preferences, improve the experience, analyze traffic, strengthen security, and provide necessary features.

You may manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. If you disable cookies, some website or service features may not work properly.

5. Third-party services

To provide, maintain, and improve our services, we may use third-party service providers to help process some data, for example:

  • website hosting and cloud infrastructure

These third-party service providers may process data only to the extent needed to provide their services, and they should process related data in accordance with their privacy policies and applicable law.

6. Sharing and disclosure of data

We do not sell your personal data.

We may share or disclose necessary data in the following circumstances:

  • sharing necessary data with service providers as needed to provide our services
  • when required by law, a regulator, a court, or lawful process

  • with your consent or at your direction

7. Data retention

We retain data for as long as needed to provide services, perform contracts, process billing, maintain security, improve services, resolve disputes, comply with the law, or enforce our rights.

Retention periods for different types of data may vary depending on the service, account status, legal requirements, tax or accounting needs, and security and operational needs.

8. Data security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data against unauthorized access, use, alteration, disclosure, loss, or destruction.

However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be 100% secure. You should keep your account, password, sign-in method, and other authentication information secure, and prevent unauthorized use of your account.

9. Your rights

Depending on the laws that apply in your region, you may have the right to request to query, review, obtain a copy of, correct, supplement, stop the collection of, stop the processing of, stop the use of, or delete personal data relating to you.

If you wish to exercise these rights, please contact us through the contact details at the end of this policy. We may need to verify your identity before we can process your request.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, operations, technical tools, or legal requirements.

When this policy is updated, the “Last updated” date at the top of the page will be updated at the same time. If you continue to use lihi services after an update, that means you have read and understood the updated content.

14. Brand monitoring and public Threads posts

TS Media Group (also referred to as “lihi”, “we”, or “us”) also operates a brand-monitoring dashboard that only authorized internal staff may sign in to. We use it to understand public discussion of our own brands on Threads. The dashboard is not open to the public and is not part of the lihi short-URL product.

14.1 Purpose

We use Meta’s official Threads API (the threads_basic and threads_keyword_search permissions) to search for public Threads posts that mention our own brands, so that staff can identify potential customers and customer complaints and can improve our services and handle those complaints. The search keywords are primarily our brand and product names, including “lihi”, “lihi.io”, “lihi 短網址”, and “貝克菜” (Beckcai). Staff may also enable a small number of related demand or competitor terms, still only for this internal monitoring purpose.

14.2 Data we collect and their source

The source of this data is public Threads posts returned by Meta’s official Threads Keyword Search API — posts that the author has already made public on Threads. We do not use this integration to read content that is not public on a private profile, and we do not collect private messages.

For public posts that match a keyword, we may store:

  • the post URL
  • the platform post identifier
  • the author’s public display name
  • the author’s public username
  • the public post text (and a title supplied by the platform or generated by our system, if any)
  • the time the post was published
  • the time we retrieved the post
  • the search keyword that matched
  • an internal AI classification and the reason for it (for example: potential customer, complaint, or general discussion)

We do not request an “owner” field or other fields that this search endpoint does not provide, and we do not separately collect your email address, phone number, or non-public profile information.

14.3 Legal basis and specific purpose

TS Media Group is a company in Taiwan. Our collection, processing, and use of the data described in this section are governed by Taiwan’s Personal Data Protection Act and related regulations.

The specific purposes are: protecting our brand reputation, identifying potential customers, handling customer complaints, and improving our services. The data we process are mainly personal data that the individual has made public on Threads, or that are otherwise lawfully public. We process these data only to the extent necessary for the purposes above.

14.4 What we do not do

With respect to the Threads data described in this section, we will not:

  • publish, reply, quote, repost, or send private messages on Threads through this application
  • sell these data, or share them with third parties for those parties’ own marketing or reuse (except where Section 6 of this policy already allows disclosure, such as when required by law, legal process, or to protect security)
  • use these data to deliver personalized advertising, or to build a profile for external use
  • display these data on a public website, in a plugin, or in a search feature available to customers

Access to the brand-monitoring dashboard is limited to authorized internal staff.

14.5 Retention

Public Threads posts that match a keyword, together with their classification data, are in principle kept for 12 months from the date we retrieved them, and are deleted when that period ends. If a record is still needed to handle a complaint, a dispute, or a legal requirement, we may keep it for as long as that need lasts and will delete it when the need ends.

If you make a valid deletion request, or if we receive a Meta data-deletion callback that we can match to stored records, we will delete the data or explain any reason we cannot do so, as described in Section 14.7, without waiting for the retention period to end.

To complete a deletion request and to let you check its status, we will keep a request record and confirmation code for 12 months. Those records will not include the deleted content.

14.6 Security and access limits

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the data in this section against unauthorized access, use, alteration, disclosure, loss, or destruction. The brand-monitoring dashboard is access-controlled and may be used only by authorized internal staff. Credentials used to call the Threads API are kept in a controlled server environment and are not published on our websites or shown to the public.

Because of how our services and cloud infrastructure are operated, the data may be transferred, stored, or processed outside Taiwan, as described in Section 11 of this policy.

14.7 Access, deletion, and other requests

Under the Personal Data Protection Act, you may request to query, review, obtain a copy of, correct, supplement, stop the collection of, stop the processing of, stop the use of, or delete personal data relating to you. We may need to verify your identity or the posts involved before we can act on a request.

You may submit a request in either of the following ways:

  1. Official customer-service channel (manual request)
    Please use the official customer-service channel listed in the “Contact us” section of this policy: “Contact lihi Support” (https://lihistatus.com/contact), or email service@lihi.io. Please put “Threads data deletion / access request” in the subject or body, and where possible include your public Threads username and the URL of the relevant public post so that we can find the record.
  2. Meta data-deletion callback
    If you request deletion of your data through your Meta or Threads app settings, Meta will send a verified request to our data-deletion callback:
    https://lihi-brand-monitor.monster7405.workers.dev/threads/data-deletion
    We will verify the request according to Meta’s specifications and start deletion. You can check progress on the status page below, using the confirmation code returned by that callback:
    https://lihi-brand-monitor.monster7405.workers.dev/threads/deletion-status
    Please note: public post data returned by Threads keyword search does not include the author’s account ID. For posts published by other users and retrieved through keyword search, we usually cannot record the author’s ID, so such posts cannot be matched and deleted automatically through this callback. If you want us to delete such posts, please use option 1 above and email service@lihi.io with the URLs of the relevant public posts, and we will locate and handle them manually.

If a member of staff or any other Threads user who has authorized this application removes the application in their Meta or Threads settings, we will discard the stored access credential and stop using it.

We will complete a valid, complete request within 15 days of receiving it. If necessary, we may extend this period by 15 days, and we will notify you of the extension. If a request is incomplete or we still need to verify identity, the time limit starts on the day after the missing information is provided.
